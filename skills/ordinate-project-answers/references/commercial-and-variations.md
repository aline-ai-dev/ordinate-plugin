# Commercial matters and variations

Read this for variations, claims, quotes, costs, payment, procurement status, and approvals with money attached.

## There is no variations register

No tool returns a variations or change register. Build the list from:

- Contracts and their terms: `searchContracts`, then `inspectDocument` and `readDocumentPage` for the clauses that govern changes and payment.
- Email: `searchEmails` and `grepEmails` for the variation reference, the instruction, quotes and approvals. Read whole threads.
- Meeting reports: commercial items and decisions recorded there.
- Worklist: `queryAiduWorklist` with the decision and issue types.
- Procurement: `queryProcurement` for item status, quotes received and approvals, with `item_evidence` for the sources behind an item.

Say that the list is assembled from these sources and may be incomplete, because a missing variation can cost the client money.

## Keep the stage exact

For each variation or commercial item, report the stage the evidence proves:

- raised or requested
- quoted or submitted
- under review
- approved or instructed (say by whom)
- rejected or withdrawn
- valued, certified or paid

"Submitted" is not "approved". An approval in principle is not an instruction. If the latest message only shows a submission, say "submitted, no approval found in the sources searched".

## Figures

- Quote amounts with currency, and whether they include tax, as the source states them.
- When two sources give different figures for the same item, show both with their sources and dates. Do not average or pick one silently.
- Do not total amounts across items unless every figure is at the same stage and basis, and say what the total covers.

## Answer shape

- Headline: how many items were found, their total stage picture, and the main risk.
- A table or list per item: reference, description, party as Organisation (Person), stage, amount, source and date.
- End with "Evidence and gaps": sources searched, attachments not readable (quotes are often attached), and conflicting figures.
