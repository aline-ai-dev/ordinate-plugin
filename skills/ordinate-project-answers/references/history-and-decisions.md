# History and decisions

Read this for "what happened", "when did", "why was it decided", timelines and meeting outcomes.

## There is no decisions register

No tool returns a decisions register. Decisions are found in:

- Full meeting reports, which record what was agreed. Prefer them over pre-reads and agendas, which record only what was proposed for discussion.
- Email threads where a decision is stated or confirmed.
- `queryAiduWorklist` with the decision type. Confirm each item in its source email or meeting report, because a worklist item is a summary, not the record.
- Formal records: instructions, approvals, signed minutes.

Say that the history is assembled from these sources and may be incomplete.

## Build the timeline from first statements

- Date each event by when it happened, from the source that first records it. A recent email that quotes older messages is not evidence that the event was recent.
- Read full threads with `readEmailThread`, paging until there is no more, so later replies that reverse or confirm a decision are not missed.
- Sort events by event date. If a source gives only its own publication date, say the event happened on or before that date.
- When two sources give different dates for the same event, show both.

## Decided versus discussed

Keep these apart, because a reader may act on a decision that was only proposed:

- Proposed or discussed: raised in a pre-read, agenda or email, no agreement recorded.
- Agreed: recorded as agreed in a meeting report or confirmed in email by the party with authority.
- Instructed or formalised: issued as an instruction, approval or contract change.
- Reversed or superseded: a later source changes it.

Name who decided as Organisation (Person), and cite the record.

## Answer shape

- Headline: the decision or event and its date, or the current position if it changed over time.
- A dated timeline, each line with its source.
- The reasons recorded in the sources, separated from any inference.
- End with "Evidence and gaps": sources and date ranges searched, unreadable attachments, and conflicting dates.
